02 / MANDATE

A coordination layer,
not another authority.

The protocol exists to reduce information asymmetry: the owner retains control, the miner retains choice, and every important claim leaves verifiable evidence. RescueMesh neither promises confirmation nor attempts to replace consensus.

Active network — The prototype accepts test environments only.
Mainnet — Locked in both configuration and execution.
Valid announcements — Signed, short-lived, and bounded.
Public jobs — Commitments and aggregate values only.

03 Protocol

Four steps. No point of custody.

Every step reduces the information being shared while preserving an independent verification path.

  1. 01

    Seal

    Sensitive data is encrypted locally. The private manifest never enters the public API.

    owner → sealed store
  2. 02

    Commit

    A salted root, aggregate economics, and an expiring policy are published.

    bundle → commitment
  3. 03

    Evaluate

    The miner compares verifiable value with the actual cost of displaced block space.

    gain ≥ marginal cost
  4. 04

    Prove

    Accepted work produces a signed receipt bound to the job and resistant to duplicates.

    share → proof of help

05 Security model

Trust is replaced by boundaries.

Controls are explicit, testable, and closed by default. Known limitations are part of the product, not hidden in the fine print.

CTRL / 01

Local interface

Administration is bound to loopback. Writes require authorization and constant-time comparison.

CTRL / 02

Minimal surface

There is no raw transaction endpoint. Size, frequency, methods, and fields are restricted.

CTRL / 03

Separated cryptography

AES-256-GCM encryption, Ed25519 signatures, and distinct secrets reduce impact and prevent reuse.

CTRL / 04

Impossible activation

Mainnet and broadcast are unimplemented. No configuration flag can enable them accidentally.

Current boundary Declared risk

Hidden-template validity

A miner cannot fully validate transactions that remain sealed. Until zero-disclosure validity proofs exist, the miner must use a trusted local coordinator or reject the job.
